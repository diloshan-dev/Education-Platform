# A/L Education Platform - Sri Lanka 🎓📚
> **Master your A/L exam one past paper at a time.**

Welcome to the **A/L Education Platform**, an interactive web portal designed specifically for Advanced Level (A/L) students in Sri Lanka. This platform provides easy access to past papers, model papers, and official marking schemes for technology and general subjects.

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Past Paper Store & Downloads:** Access and purchase past papers, model papers, and marking schemes.
* **Subject Coverage:** Optimized content for ICT, SFT, ET, GIT, General English (GE), and General Knowledge (GK).
* **User Authentication:** Complete Login and Sign Up workflow for students (`login.html`, `signup.html`).
* **Interactive Portal:** Organized paper browsing system and user profile management (`papers.html`, `profile.js`).
* **Responsive Layout:** Clean design built with modern HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
